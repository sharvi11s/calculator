MATH MAGIC

Can you predict 1089?

Follow these steps with a three-digit number. MyCalc has a prediction for you!

Let's try it

  1. Choose any three-digit number whose first and last digits differ by at least 2.
  2. Reverse its digits to make a new number.
  3. Subtract the smaller number from the larger one.
  4. Reverse the result. If it has only two digits, add a leading zero first.
  5. Add the result and its reverse together.

Your answer is 1089! ✨

DID YOU KNOW?

The curiosity corner.

Three little facts that make math more interesting.

∞

Infinity has levels.

There are different sizes of infinity. The real numbers are uncountable, while the integers are countable.

0.999...

It really equals one.

The repeating decimal 0.999... is exactly equal to 1, not just very close to it.

π

Pi never repeats.

Pi is irrational, which means its decimal digits go on forever without repeating in a fixed pattern.
